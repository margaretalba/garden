#digital-garden 

From Carolyn Yoo's [Intro to Digital Gardening](https://cyoo.substack.com/p/intro-to-digital-gardening-a-quietly)

### 5 reasons to start a digital garden
1. It helps you reclaim your attention from social media
2. It helps you remember what you consume
3. It helps you form your opinions in public
4. It helps you think non-linearly and non-chronologically
5. It helps you recycle and combine ideas

### How to create your own

 [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/)
   
   >**073126** I've been using Obsidian for a few months now as almost like a diary. Looking back, I've been severely underutilizing it. Still to this day, I feel like I'm truly not unlocking its potential. I think the biggest obstacle for is the amount of customization. It's so overwhelming, especially for someone who is used to writing in journals and straightforward blog formats. I'm excited to use it a digital garden and personal wiki though.
