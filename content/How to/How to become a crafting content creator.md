As much as I love a short-form video, I want to try my hand at substack and YouTube first, just because they have been fun for me to consume lately. After months of just watching videos and reading articles, I want to play in the sand box too.
#### Lean Into What You Have to Offer (And It Doesn’t Need to Be the Perfect Craft Room)

I need to make one thing clear off the bat: you do not need the perfect craft room, best supplies, or even a lot of skill to begin posting craft content.

Here are some content focus ideas that have nothing to do with how aesthetically pleasing your craft room is:

- Share nap time projects for new moms longing to use their hands for more than runny noses and dirty diapers
- Let your sense of humor or interesting editing style set you apart
- Turn newbies into accomplished makers with very in-depth tutorials
- Combine your love of history and making by focusing on the tradition behind your craft
- The nice thing about being a crafting content creator is, your taste is a niche of its own!

#### Other Crafting Content Ideas

- A craft-a-thon, [quilt-a-thon](https://www.tiktok.com/@emmaheathkelly/video/7429039444541410603), stitch-a-thon, anything-a-thon!
- Your favorite supplies sources
- “If I had to recreate my entire knitting toolbox from scratch, here’s what I would rebuy again”
- Misconceptions about your chosen craft (mixed with funny correct assumptions)
- WIP Wednesday, of course!
- “[Save these dimensions](https://www.tiktok.com/@emmaheathkelly/video/7426448460662050091) to recreate this ___ project”
- Roundups of past projects by type
- Let followers pick your next project from a few options
- Make dupes of trending brands (Big guys only! Not small businesses)
- Join a craft club and share what you are making there! And what other club makers are working on too
- Try your hand at aspirational crafts or dying art forms
- Plan a maker swap! This is a great way to share platforms with another creator
- Think of crafting-adjacent ideas, like filming your visit to a craft market or a trip that inspires your next project
#### Turn Every Project Into Multiple Engaging Posts
Here are ideas for pieces of content you can pull from every project:

- Intro to your planned project and how what method you are going to try
- “[New project loading](https://www.tiktok.com/t/ZP8b4sShR/)”
- Buying the supplies, picking the palette, gathering inspo, or other prep work
- Multiple steps you are working on in real time with voiceover explanations
- Make with me - a peaceful video of working on your project
- Learn with me - take people along on learning the needed skills for this project as you do it
- A little ASMR
- Best [dopamine hit step](https://www.tiktok.com/@emmaheathkelly/video/7480273597911584043?_r=1&_t=ZP-94zbFrxqSvn) of that project
- Project reveal quick [no talking](https://www.tiktok.com/@emmaheathkelly/video/7424589890941242666)
- Project reveal [with talking](https://www.tiktok.com/@emmaheathkelly/video/7423400064594234666)
- Full process in short aesthetic clips with music
- Full process with voiceover in one video
- Basic tutorial in single video
- Most bare-bone info about the project in [one saveable video](https://www.tiktok.com/t/ZP8b4tbF3/)
- In-depth tutorial in multiple steps
- A small tip or step that made this project so much better
- Revealing the project to the person you made it for or where it ended up in your home
