### 3 Part structure
1. Intro
	- Hook, present the premise of the mini essay
	- A sentence or two is fine.
	- Intro goals
		- Make the premise clear
		- Spark curiosity
	- Types
		- Make a strong statement
		- Inspire emotion
		- Ask a question
		- Tell a story. Take the reader in the middle of the action. You can still use this for non fiction.
2. Body
3. Conclusion
	- Could be a sentence or two
