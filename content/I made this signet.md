Making this signet with blender, wax
Hi, I'm Margaret and I'm making this flipping wax signet.
I love signets, I actually made and engraved this one with my dog's star a few years ago. But I want to make a new one. You see, I've been writing letters more often and I've grown a small collection of wax seals. 

I know there are wax seal signets, but it wasn't until I came across this photo on pinterest that made me think, what if it was a flipping wax signet??

#### Step 1: Create the body

So first, I need to create the body. 

I like to use the program Blender for 3D rendering. It's free and open source so there's plugins for virtually everything. There's also sooo many tutorials on youtube for almost everything you'd want to make. 

**Tip #1** Always have a set of calipers nearby. You can use this side to measure inside any rings. I used to make things without calipers and they would always turn out either too big or too small. Having a set of calipers anchors you.

**Tip #2**
 If you have a 3D printer, make prototypes. I have a bambu p1s, so it's not great for small items like rings. There's lots of ridges and sometimes misses details. So sometimes I scale it larger. I'm not looking for details as much as the mechanics and the form.
