### Types of Cognitive Biases
- Anchoring bias
	- The first piece of information consumers encounter heavily influences their subsequent judgements
- Scarcity bias
	- FOMO, LTOs
- Availability heuristic
	- Consumers make decisions based on how easily informations comes to mind (like Youtube ads for me!)
	- Why its important to have an omnichannel approach
- The bandwagon effect
	- People are more likely to do or believe something if many other people do or believe the same
	- Positive reviews
	- UGC
- Loss aversion
