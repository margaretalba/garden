#digital-marketing

Digital Marketing needs to be paired with inbound marketing.

**Inbound marketing**
A methodology that focuses on attracting customers by creating valuable and relevant content tailored to their needs.


More personalized to audiences
Think of the entire consumer journey
Be omni channel - focuses on a consistent brand experience on all channels

Digital consumer journey
Path is not linear, and the consumer is participating in different channels

Trust is paramount

Motivation
- Intrinsic: Comes from within
- Extrinsic: Responds to external rewards

### Attitudes can be shaped cognitively, emotionally, and behaviorially
Cognitive: reviews
Emotionally: Social media
Behaviorially: buy experience

### Types of Cognitive Biases
- Anchoring bias
- Scarcity bias
- Availability heuristic
- The bandwagon effect
- Loss aversion
