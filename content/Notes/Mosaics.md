Goodwin, E. M. (2000). _Classic mosaic: Designs and projects inspired by 6,000 years of mosaic art_. Trafalgar Square Publishing.

**Equipment**
Storing
- Tools - should be kept dry and stored together in boxes or drawers for easy use
- Adhesives, cements, and grouts - should be kept in a seperate area, perhaps a low shelf, and should avoid contact with any form of damp condition
Floor
- Should be cleaned every day, dust can go everywhere and anywhere. Must have windows or dust extractors
