Hi, I'm Margaret and I have now reached a year without Instagram

I know for many of you, this doesn't sound like a big deal. My husband doesn't have social media at all.  But as a creative, Instagram felt vital to have, at least in a marketing and visibility perspective - it's the main reason why I sold out of my first collection ever. It was all Instagram.

**So why did I deactivate my Instagram?**

I've been feeling resentful towards Instagram for a while - as much as it brought me closer and even introduced me to new people, I hated the ads, I hated how I could get lost in it, I hated some of the feelings it brought in me

But it wasn't until I got pregnant that it came to a head.  I had pretty severe anxiety when I was pregnant. Like scared to go outside because I believed something terrible would happen to me and I would lose the one thing I wanted for years: a baby. I was somehow convinced that because I wanted this baby so badly, the universe would take it away from me. I braced myself for the worst scenarios and Instagram, literally, made it worse. 

It wasn't long until I was flooded with videos on what I NEEDED to do, what I had to buy. It was truly too much.

And then when I actually gave birth to my daughter, the videos, if you can believe it, got worse. Plus, I made the decision to not post her at all. Not her name, her face, anything. Which I still stand by.

But then something weird happened. I would be nursing at 4 am, go on my phone and see all my friends' stories - the summer travels, the events. I was hit with a FOMO, and wanted to share my own things - but at that point every single photo was of my daughter.

So I had nothing to share.

After months of these, I decided to just deactivate it.

**What happened next**

The habit to go on instagram was still there. I found myself looking it up on my phone just from muscle memory. 

The second thing I noticed is that I went to other forms of dopamine hits. For me it was Youtube shorts. MY GOD. I think it's KIND of getting better, but the alghortithm for Youtube was just so not as aligned as my Instagram and Tiktok was.
