HOW TO DOWNLOAD TEMPLATER
1. Open Obsidian
2. Go into Settings > Community plugins
3. Under Community Plugins click browse
4. Search Templater

HOW TO FIX LINKS IN QUARTZ/OBSIDIAN
1. Go into quartz.config.yaml (I use Visual Studio code)
2. Look for Plugin.CrawlLinks
3. Make sure it says:
   `Plugin.CrawlLinks({ markdownLinkResolution: "shortest" }),`
4. Save!
